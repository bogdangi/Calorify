📝 User Story: Segmentation & Multi-Food Recognition (V4)

Title: Segmentation & Multi-Food
Feature: Segment multiple foods per photo with per-food portion estimation and total calories
User Story:
• As a photo-logging user with a full plate,
• I want multiple foods and their portions recognized from one photo,
• So that a single photo yields a realistic total calorie estimate.

Status: 🔜 Future (deliberately deferred; advanced stage).

⚙️ Acceptance Criteria

1. Pipeline: Photo → Segmentation → Multiple foods → Per-food portion estimation → Nutrition DB → Total calories.
2. All stages stay on-device, modular, lazily loaded, and confirmable by the user before saving.
